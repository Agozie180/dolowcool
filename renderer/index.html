Before we start

Dolowcool uses your camera to estimate pulse and breathing rate (via the Presage SmartSpectra SDK) while this window is open. It compares them to your own calm baseline and only decides whether Send is locked. It does not diagnose anything or judge your emotions. Dolowcool stores no video or readings, and SDK telemetry is off by default. Your API key is still used to authenticate with Presage. You can stop at any time.
