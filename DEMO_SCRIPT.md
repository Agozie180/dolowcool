# Dolowcool demo script

Target length: **75–90 seconds**. Record the real SmartSpectra source, not the simulator.

## Shot list

1. **The problem — 5 seconds**
   - Start on the consent screen.
   - Voiceover: “The worst time to send a message is often the moment you most want to.”

2. **Consent and privacy — 10 seconds**
   - Show the unchecked consent control.
   - Select it and begin the session.
   - Point out “Telemetry off” and the live camera indicator.

3. **Real signal and baseline — 15 seconds**
   - Show the live preview, framing guide, pulse, and breathing rate.
   - Let the continuous baseline complete.
   - Voiceover: “There is no universal calm number. Dolowcool learns mine.”

4. **The lock moment — 20 seconds**
   - Type a believable emotionally charged reply.
   - Raise pulse or breathing naturally and safely, such as after brief movement away from the desk.
   - Show the gauge cross the lock marker.
   - Highlight the plain-language explanation naming the signal driving the pause.

5. **Settle — 15 seconds**
   - Sit comfortably and breathe normally.
   - Show the uninterrupted eight-second hold counting down.
   - Briefly obstruct the camera once to demonstrate that signal loss resets rather than bypasses the hold.

6. **Choice, not control — 15 seconds**
   - Show the action unlocking.
   - Open the message in the default email client, emphasizing that nothing sends automatically.
   - Briefly show the deliberate override and Save draft options.

7. **Close — 5 seconds**
   - End on “Write now. Send when settled.”

## Recording checklist

- The video visibly uses the **Live SmartSpectra** source.
- Pulse and breathing values change on screen.
- The camera preview is visible without showing private background details.
- The lock, signal-loss reset, calm hold, and unlock are all visible.
- No API key, notification, email address, or private draft content appears.
